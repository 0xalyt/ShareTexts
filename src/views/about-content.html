Why it exists

The little gap
between screens.

The photo is on your phone. The form is on your laptop. The link you need is on the other screen.

ShareTexts is for that moment. Open it on both devices, connect them, and send what you need. iPhone to Windows. Android to Mac. Your devices don’t need to match.

It is a utility, not another place to keep up with.

01

Start with what you have.

A modern browser and an internet connection on both devices. No installation and no new account.

02

Send what you need.

A sentence, a link, a photo, a video, or a document. Pick a file or paste what’s on your clipboard.

03

Know what stays.

Live rooms, Temporary Spaces, and saved items have different lifetimes. That difference should be clear.

Without the fine print

Private isn’t
a vague promise.

Read the privacy policy ↗

Live rooms

Transfers are encrypted in transit, directly between devices when possible, with an encrypted relay fallback. The service issues the room secret, so this is not zero knowledge. Rooms expire unless you enable Stay Connected.

Temporary Spaces

Content is stored server-side for the lifetime you choose, from 6 hours to 7 days, or until the space closes early. Keep your own copy of anything you need.

Saved items

Items you choose to save stay in this browser until you remove them or clear site data. They do not sync to other devices.
