What’s new

A little easier to share.

Small improvements to the handoff between your devices.

Public beta. Free to use. No account needed. This is a release note, not a live uptime report. Tell Garv about a problem.

22 November 2026

  • On supported installed browsers, choose ShareTexts from your phone’s share sheet to bring in text, photos and files. Review them before sending. Up to 20 files and 100 MB per share.
  • Installation is offered after two items have actually arrived, not on your first visit.
  • Recent devices are easier to find when you come back. A remembered device is not necessarily online.
  • Received files announce their sender to screen readers. Keyboard focus is easier to see in the composer.
  • The launch interface is in English. Other languages will return when their translations have been reviewed.

Sharing from your phone

Install ShareTexts from a supported browser, then look for ShareTexts in another app’s Share menu. Availability depends on your phone and browser; if it is missing, open ShareTexts and choose the files instead.

Items from the share sheet wait in a temporary inbox in this browser. Opening the share clears that inbox. Unopened items expire after ten minutes and are cleared when another share arrives. Nothing is sent to another device until you choose Send.
