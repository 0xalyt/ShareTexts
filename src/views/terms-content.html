Using ShareTexts

ShareTexts is a free browser tool for sharing content between devices. You do not need an account. By using the service, you agree to these terms and to follow the laws that apply to you.

The Privacy Policy explains how live rooms, Temporary Spaces, Hop, and Saved Items handle your content.

Your content is yours

You keep your rights to the text, photos, videos, and files you share. ShareTexts claims no ownership of them. Share only content you have the right to send.

You are responsible for what you send and who receives it. A room or space invitation gives access to whoever holds it. Only approve Nearby invitations and link Hop devices you recognize.

Encryption in transit does not make the service unable to access live-room or Temporary Space content. Read the Privacy Policy before sharing sensitive material.

Acceptable use

Do not use ShareTexts to:

  • Send content that is illegal where you live.
  • Harass, threaten, or spam anyone.
  • Bypass pairing, access controls, or rate limits, or disrupt the service.

Rooms are temporary

Live rooms expire automatically unless you enable Stay Connected. Keep both devices connected while transferring, and check that a file has finished before closing a tab.

Temporary Spaces store content on the server for the chosen lifetime, from 6 hours to 7 days, or until the creator closes them. Hop uses a server mailbox for encrypted items with a 30-day item lifetime. Neither feature is a permanent backup.

Saved Items stay in the browser where you save them, until you remove them or browser data is cleared. Closing a room or space does not delete copies already saved on someone's device. Keep your own backup.
