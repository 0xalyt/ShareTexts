ShareTexts
Send text, links, photos and files between your devices.

Open sharetexts.online on both devices. Choose Send on one, then use
the code or QR on the other. No account or app download is needed.

Live-room transfers are encrypted in transit, using a direct connection
when available or an encrypted relay. The service issues room secrets;
this is not a zero-knowledge system. Temporary Spaces store content
until closed or expired, for up to seven days. Hop keeps encrypted
items for linked devices for up to 30 days. Saved Items stay in the
browser where you save them.
