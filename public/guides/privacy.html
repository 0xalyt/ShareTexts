Know where your content stays

A practical guide to live rooms, server-stored spaces, Hop, and the copies saved in your browser.

Start with how you are sharing

A live room, Temporary Space, Hop mailbox, and Saved Items library do different jobs. Do not assume they store content the same way.

  • Live room: share while devices are connected. Transfers are encrypted in transit, with a relay when needed. The service issues the room secret and could derive its key.
  • Temporary Space: server-stored content for later, from 6 hours to 7 days or until the creator closes it. Anyone holding a working code or link can join.
  • Hop: encrypted items wait for linked devices in a server mailbox with a 30-day item lifetime. Your browser encrypts content before uploading it.
  • Saved Items: an explicit local copy in the browser where you saved it. These items do not sync to your other devices.

Choose what you keep

Download a received file to keep it outside ShareTexts. Remove Saved Items you no longer need, or clear the library. Clearing browser site data can remove local history, saved copies, and device keys.

Keep your original file until you have opened the received copy. Browser storage can be cleared by private browsing, storage pressure, or device cleanup.

Choose who gets access

Share codes and links only with intended recipients. Accept Nearby invitations from devices you recognize. When linking Hop, compare the same four emoji on both screens before approving.

Closing a room or space stops that access but cannot delete copies someone already saved. For the full policy, including connection information and activity counters, read the Privacy Policy.
