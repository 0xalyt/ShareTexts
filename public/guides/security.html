Share with the right expectations

Understand encryption, invitations, server storage, and local copies before sending something sensitive.

Encryption does not answer every privacy question

Live-room transfers are encrypted in transit, directly when possible or through an encrypted relay. The service creates the room secret and could derive the same encryption key. This is not a zero-knowledge system.

Do not use live rooms or Temporary Spaces for secrets you cannot entrust to the service. The Privacy Policy describes what the service can see.

Keep invitations private

  • A live-room code, QR code, or link lets another device connect while the room is available.
  • A working Temporary Space code or link lets someone join and access its content.
  • Approve Nearby invitations only from devices you recognize.
  • For Hop, compare the four emoji on both screens before approving a new linked device.

Do not post invitations in a public screenshot or support request. If access has been shared with the wrong person, close the room or space.

Understand the storage mode

  • Live rooms: the service helps connect and relay transfers, but does not keep a server-side message history.
  • Temporary Spaces: text and links are stored on the server until closure or the chosen expiry, from 6 hours to 7 days. Deletion runs after access ends.
  • Hop: the browser encrypts content before upload. Encrypted copies wait in a server mailbox with a 30-day item lifetime. The content key stays with your devices.
  • Saved Items: copies you explicitly save stay in that browser. They do not sync, and the library has no separate ShareTexts storage encryption.

Protect the receiving device too

Someone using your browser profile may be able to open local history and Saved Items. On a shared computer, remove saved copies and clear site data when finished. Clearing site data can also remove Hop keys and require linking again.

Closing a room or space does not erase copies already downloaded by another device. Keep an original file until you have checked that the received copy opens.
