Choose how to share

Use a live room for a handoff now, a Temporary Space for later, or Hop for your linked devices.

Both devices are ready now

Open ShareTexts on each device. Choose Send on the first and Receive on the other. Enter the current six-digit code, scan the QR code, or open the room link.

Type a note, paste a link, or attach a file. Keep both tabs open and the devices awake until it finishes. Download the file on the receiving device before closing.

Someone will open it later

Create a Temporary Space with a lifetime from 6 hours to 7 days. Share its eight-character code or link. Members can return to add or copy content without the creator keeping a browser open.

A space stores content on the server. Anyone with a working invitation can join. Access ends at closure; server deletion runs afterward. Keep copies of anything you need.

You share with your own devices regularly

Open Hop from the home screen and link your other device. Compare the same four emoji on both screens before approving. Hop can keep encrypted items waiting while a linked device is offline, with a 30-day item lifetime.

What happens during a live transfer

The connection service introduces the devices. They try a direct browser-to-browser connection. If that route is unavailable, an encrypted relay carries the transfer. A shared Wi-Fi network is not required for code or link pairing.

The service issues the room secret and could derive its encryption key. Encryption in transit does not mean the service cannot read a live transfer. Read the Privacy Policy before sharing sensitive content.

Save is a separate step

A received item is not automatically a file in Downloads. Use its download control, or explicitly save it in ShareTexts's Saved Items library. Saved Items stay in that browser and are not a backup across devices.
