# Browser marks

Unmodified SVGs from [Browser Logos](https://github.com/alrra/browser-logos/tree/58881b84c4d73adc03c06fa2c275a7abee02d935/src), pinned to commit `58881b84c4d73adc03c06fa2c275a7abee02d935`. Includes Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Brave, Samsung Internet, Opera and Vivaldi. Grayscale is applied in CSS, not baked into the artwork.

Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, Brave and Samsung Internet logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners. They identify compatible browsers, not endorsements. Grayscale is applied in CSS; the source artwork is unchanged. The collection's MIT license is included alongside the assets.
