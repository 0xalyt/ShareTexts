# Relaunch cut — one photograph, six beats

Owner note: the first cut explained the mechanism (code, QR, six digits) and
read like a setup guide. A launch film has to say what the product does for
the viewer, why it is better than the route they use today, and how they come
out ahead — the mechanism is proof, not the pitch.

## What the article says, and where it lands in this cut

Source: Andrey Chernyshuk, *How to make launch videos if you are a web
designer (try storyboarding)* — storyboard like a website (flow → main goal →
CTA → sections), chop the dashboard into bite-sized pieces, keep contrast
between sections, and earn the first seconds with a bold promise.

| Lesson | Beat |
|---|---|
| First 3 s carry the whole promise, legible muted | Beat 1 is fully settled at frame 0 and is also the poster |
| One idea per shot; never a whole dashboard | Every beat shows one object: a photo, three struck rows, one screen, three cards, one URL |
| Chop the UI into pieces | The room UI appears only as a received photo, a saved chip and three payload cards — no sidebar, no thread |
| Contrast between beats | Promise (big type left) → refusal (list) → arrival (full-bleed image) → inventory (cards) → trust (type + fragment) → door |
| End on what changes for the viewer | "It's already there." and "No email to yourself. No sync to wait for." |

## The six beats (15.0 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, silent)

| # | Time | Picture | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–2.7 | Phone and laptop; the photo crosses the wire | `Phone to laptop.` / `Nothing in between.` / NO ACCOUNT · NO APP · NO INSTALL |
| 2 | 2.7–5.4 | Three routes struck through, one at a time | `Everything else makes a copy.` → `ShareTexts doesn't.` |
| 3 | 5.4–8.1 | The photo lands full-bleed on the laptop, light sweeps it, `Saved on this device` | `It's already there.` / `One code on each device. That's the whole setup.` |
| 4 | 8.1–10.8 | Text, link and a 24.3 MB file arrive as three cards; the file completes | `Text. Links. Photos. Files.` / `Sent as they are.` |
| 5 | 10.8–13.2 | URL with the fragment key bracketed | `Encrypted end to end.` / `The key is made on your devices.` |
| 6 | 13.2–15.0 | Lockup | `Move anything between your devices.` · sharetexts.online · Free. No account. |

## What changed from v1

- The code/QR plate is no longer the spine. One sentence in beat 3 carries it.
- The chapter rail (01 THE PROMISE … 06 YOUR TURN) is gone: it made the film
  read as a deck. A 3 px ember progress line replaces it and gives the loop a
  clock.
- The file progress card is no longer its own beat; it is one card among the
  three things that travel.
- Copy is shorter: the longest line on screen is eight words.

## Claims

All reused and already audited in `brag-output/brag-plan.md`: no account, no
app, no install; six-digit room; text/link/photo/file; byte-for-byte files;
end-to-end encryption with the key in the fragment. "Everything else makes a
copy" is a description of email drafts, drive uploads and chat compression,
not a benchmark. No numbers, logos or testimonials of third parties.
